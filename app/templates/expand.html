How this works

serde_derive is a proc-macro crate: its modules are private behind two entry points, and a proc-macro crate cannot be linked as an ordinary dependency. The build copies the pinned tree and applies five edits to one file — taking proc_macro2’s token stream instead of the compiler’s — leaving the other {{ untouched_lines }} lines exactly as published. Nothing here reimplements anything.

Every case on this page is expanded natively in CI and diffed against a committed transcript, then expanded again out of this wasm module and diffed against the same file. What you are reading is asserted to be what the compiler would really generate.

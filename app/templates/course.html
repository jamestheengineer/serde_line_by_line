The course track

The same annotations as the reference track, ordered by what has to be understood first. {{ total_units }} units, {{ course_annotations }} annotations drawn from across the annotated crates.

Units 1–14 are read out of serde_core, which teaches lifetimes, trait design and macro_rules! better than almost any crate in the ecosystem, and teaches ordinary imperative Rust hardly at all. Units 15–21 are read out of serde_derive and are about writing a procedural macro: spans, an AST of your own, an attribute language, and inferring the bounds of an impl you are generating. From unit 22 the source is serde_json, the first of the three that is a format: its units are about keeping the contracts the first fourteen declared, in a hand-written parser, with numbers, errors, feature gates and a little unsafe. Units no crate can supply are written from scratch and labelled supplementary rather than dressed up as something they demonstrate.

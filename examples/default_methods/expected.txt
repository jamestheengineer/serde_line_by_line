  Tuned     {elapsed: 4265, celsius: 21.5, note: -}   [serialize_struct(len = 2), skip_field("note")]
  Verbose recorded no skip_field: its default body is just Ok(())

Notice the length in the last case. Both formats were told len = 2,
because a skipped field is not counted; Verbose then wrote two fields
and Tuned wrote three. The length says what the data structure intends
to send, not what the format decides to emit.

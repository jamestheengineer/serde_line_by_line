isize and usize have no method of their own: the `as i64` / `as u64`
tail on their invocation widens them first. The method name above is
the same on a 64-bit host and on 32-bit WASM.

i128 and u128 are the only two of the sixteen whose Serializer method
has a default body, and that body errors. Trace never overrode them.
